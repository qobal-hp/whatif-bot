All nine images use classical oil painting, soft brushwork, warm chiaroscuro, and no visible text, lettering, or captions.

1. Mark 4:37–40 — Jesus rises in a weathered fishing boat as the wind falls and lamplight touches the wet faces of his disciples.
2. Psalm 131:1–2 — A small child rests against his mother beside a glowing lamp in a quiet stone room.
3. Proverbs 3:21–24 — A traveler’s sandals rest beside a simple bed, with an evening footpath visible through the window.
4. Isaiah 30:15–18 — A robed prophet watches riders hurry down a dusty road while golden light lingers over the still hills of Judah.
5. Lamentations 3:19–24 — A solitary figure sits among Jerusalem’s ruined stones as the first light of morning enters the street.
6. Zephaniah 3:16–17 — The people of Jerusalem gather beneath a luminous dawn, their faces slowly lifting toward the light.
7. Matthew 6:31–34 — Jesus teaches families on a grassy hillside, with a woven basket and wildflowers in the foreground.
8. Philippians 4:6–7 — Paul sits in a shadowed prison room beside a small oil lamp, a rolled parchment resting near his hand.
9. 1 Peter 5:6–7 — A weary traveler sets a heavy cloth bundle beside a doorway, where warm lamplight spills onto the ground.
